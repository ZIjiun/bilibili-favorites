# Surface brief: 收藏島 (catalog page)

Scope and visitor mode: Operate. Single self-contained HTML page, one owner, desktop and phone equally. Job: find a saved video fast, or browse one topic. Constraints: single file, Traditional Chinese, themes light / beige / dark / auto, password gate, new folders must find a place on the island, daily regeneration.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The owner's taxonomy is one game-world island seen in isometric view; each group is a themed region and each folder a level node with its video count, so choosing a topic feels like picking a stage. Refuses the category default of a sidebar tree over a card grid, and the previous flat sea chart.

OWN-WORLD: Game stage-select world map drawn in SVG: one continuous organic island on the sea (beach rim, grass, a mountain ridge at the back, a meandering river, tree clumps), each group a terrain patch with one big outlined cartoon landmark on a round earthen pedestal (AI crystal tower, 程式 gear workshop, 命理 moon temple, 遊戲 volcano, 開源 open treasure chest, 學習 clock tower, 職場 office towers, 心理 lighthouse, 生活 awning shop, 感情 heart hedge arch, 娛樂 ferris wheel, 放鬆 hot-spring beach, 萌寵 tree house). Under each: an icon-plus-name badge and a pill info bar with the video count. Clean system-sans product shell around it. Light: day sea; beige: warm afternoon; dark: night sea with glowing details.

STORY: One glance shows every topic as a place on the island and how many videos it holds. Clicking a landmark selects that group; its folder chips and videos appear below. Searching lights regions with hits and shows hit counts, dims the rest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (brand, search, theme) on one 1360px column. Below, the island map fills the column width, 13 landmarks in three staggered rows (4/5/4). Under it the 總分類 chip row, folder chips for the selected group, then the thumbnail wall. Phone: a portrait island with landmarks in three columns.

FORM: Pinned whole by the user after a degraded roll was shown, then clarified with a reference image. seed: none (pinned) — "想要一個島嶼上面有各個不同區域的感覺 每個區域有各自的特色 像是遊戲裡面那種關卡式的介面"; "背景是一座島在海面上 每個主題就是一個不同的地區 你不要照抄他的"; folders stay off the map (user choice).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
